# M4 demo checklist

What M4 delivers: camera and screen share in the web client, on top of the M3 voice call. Camera with a device choice, three
quality presets, simulcast and dynacast, self-view, tiles with the speaking ring; screen share through the browser's own picker with
three presets, an "optimise for" hint and audio where the browser offers it; **viewer opt-in** (no video is received until someone
presses Watch), the stream view with its own volume (0 to 300 %, same limiter as voices), mute, quality (Auto, High, Low, Pause
video), picture in picture and fullscreen; the sharing bar with the viewer list (client-reported) and the upload estimate; permission
bits and channel switches for camera and screen share with live revocation; server-wide and per-channel bitrate caps. Built
2026-10-29. Facts, cuts and deferrals: [18 §K](../18-open-questions-and-risks.md#k-m4-build-notes-2026-10-29).

What has been run: every server rule by automated tests (grant table, channel switches, caps, revocation by the fast path and by
the reconciler), the web logic by unit and component tests, and the whole path by the **3-person media test** (real server, real
web client, real LiveKit, three headless Chromium clients on fake devices): camera publish, opt-in, simulcast layer choice by tile
size, High and Auto, **publisher upload flat from 1 to 2 viewers and falling when nobody watches (G4)**, screen share with audio,
watching it, the sharer's viewer list, the once-only notice, Pause video, Stop watching, camera and screen share revoked and
restored mid-call. **Not run anywhere: Firefox, Safari, a real screen picker, a real camera, a phone, the VPS.** Rows marked
**(VPS)** need the server, **(you)** need a person at a real browser, **(people)** need a second person on another network,
**(Firefox)** need Firefox.

## A. Local checks

```bash
pnpm install
pnpm check                                     # typecheck, lint, headers, tests, build, licences, secrets
pnpm build && sh tools/media-test/docker.sh    # the 3-person media test; needs Docker
```

- [ ] `pnpm check` is green.
- [ ] `sh tools/media-test/docker.sh` ends with `26/26 passed` (on Linux, `pnpm media-test` does the same with host networking). It prints the measured upload with one viewer, two viewers and none.
- [ ] `MEDIA_SKIP="audio|mute|deafen|restarts|network|SPEAK|centred|past 100|ring" sh tools/media-test/docker.sh` runs only the video steps (about 2 minutes).

## B. Update the deployment (VPS)

M4 needs **no new ports, no new Caddy rules and no config change**: the firewall rules from M3 (7881/tcp, 7882/udp) carry video, and the
generated `Permissions-Policy` already allows `camera` and `display-capture`. Pull, rebuild and restart as for M3.

Optional keys in `config.yaml` under `modules.voice` (defaults in brackets):

```yaml
modules:
  voice:
    camera: { maxBitrateKbps: 1700 }       # top layer of a camera; 150 to 25000
    screenshare: { maxBitrateKbps: 6000 }  # top layer of a screen share; 300 to 60000. Your lever on egress (08 §5.5)
```

A channel can lower the screen share cap with `PATCH /channels/:id {"settings": {"maxScreenshareKbps": 2500}}` (there is no admin
screen for it yet) and can turn video or screen share off with `videoAllowed` and `screenshareAllowed`.

- [ ] **(VPS)** After the update, join from two browsers; `GET /api/v1/voice/status` shows `reconcile.repairs` unchanged apart from the first minute.
- [ ] **(VPS)** A 1080p30 share watched by one other person: the VPS shows about 4 Mbps in and about 4 Mbps out (`docker stats` or `iftop`). Add a second viewer: out doubles, in does not.

## C. Camera

- [ ] The call view has a control bar: microphone, deafen, camera, share screen, leave. Share screen is absent on a phone browser.
- [ ] **(you)** Turn the camera on. The browser asks once; your own tile shows a mirrored preview. The others see your avatar, a **CAM** badge and **Watch camera**; their data use does not change until they press it.
- [ ] **(people)** Press Watch camera on the other side: the picture appears in the tile, the speaking ring still moves, tiles do not jump.
- [ ] Settings → Voice & audio → Camera: pick another camera while yours is on; it switches without leaving the call. Unplug the chosen camera: the next start falls back to the default one.
- [ ] Settings → Camera quality: Low (360p), Standard (720p), High (1080p). A preset above the server cap is greyed out with the reason (set `camera.maxBitrateKbps: 1000` to see 1080p disabled).
- [ ] "Watch other people's cameras automatically" is off by default; switched on, cameras appear without pressing Watch.
- [ ] The first time you watch anyone a banner says they can see that you are watching; "Got it" closes it, and it does not come back.
- [ ] Take **Use a camera** (VIDEO) away from yourself in the channel's permissions: within seconds your camera stops (the light goes out), the button is disabled with the reason, and it works again when the permission returns, without rejoining.
- [ ] Tab to the tiles: one tab stop, arrow keys move between tiles.

## D. Screen share

- [ ] Share screen → dialog (quality, optimise for, share audio) → Continue → the browser's own picker. Cancel the picker: nothing happens and no error shows.
- [ ] The sharing bar appears above the call (and stays when you open a text channel): **LIVE**, what you share (entire screen, window or tab), who watches, upload estimate. The estimate says nothing about viewers because it does not depend on them.
- [ ] **(people)** The others see a tile "X is sharing their screen" with **Watch stream**, and nothing downloads. After Watch: the stream view opens (focus layout) with volume, mute, quality, picture in picture, fullscreen, Stop watching; the sharing bar says who is watching within a few seconds, and Stop watching removes them.
- [ ] Watching a second stream switches to the grid layout; Focus/Grid in the header switches back; Enter on a stream tile opens it.
- [ ] Quality: Pause video stops the download (check the browser's network panel or `chrome://webrtc-internals`), Auto brings it back; High and Low change the picture size.
- [ ] Change (sharing bar) → pick Low upload while live: no new picker, the picture stays up, the estimate changes. Audio can only be changed by stopping and starting again.
- [ ] Stop sharing from the browser's own "Stop sharing" bubble: the sharing bar and the tile disappear for everyone.
- [ ] Take **Share screen** away mid-share: the share stops within seconds and the button is disabled with the reason.
- [ ] Turn the channel switch `screenshareAllowed: false` on mid-share: same.

## E. Screen share audio, per browser (document what you see)

| Browser | Expected |
|---|---|
| **(you)** Chrome or Edge, share a **tab**, "Also share tab audio" ticked | Viewers hear it on its own slider, separate from your voice |
| **(you)** Chrome or Edge on **Windows**, share the **entire screen**, "Also share system audio" ticked | System audio, including sounds of the call itself unless the call is muted in the OS mixer: expect an echo of other people's voices |
| **(you)** Chrome or Edge on **macOS or Linux**, entire screen or window | No audio offered by the browser |
| **(Firefox)** any source | Firefox's picker has no audio option: the stream has no audio and the stream view shows no volume control |
| Safari | Not tested; no tab audio expected |

- [ ] Stream audio volume: 0 to 300 %, separate from the sharer's voice slider. Above 100 % the label says "boosted". The value is remembered per sharer.
- [ ] Stream audio is stereo (music has both channels). Mute on the stream mutes only the stream.
- [ ] Deafen: stream audio stops too; undeafen brings it back only for streams you are still watching.

## F. Other browsers and machines

- [ ] **(Firefox)** Camera, share screen (video), watch both, picture in picture (Firefox has its own button), Pause video.
- [ ] **(you)** Edge on Windows: the same as Chrome.
- [ ] **(you)** A phone browser: Watch works; Share screen is absent; the camera works if the OS lets the page use it.
- [ ] **(people)** A viewer on a network that blocks UDP: video over TCP 7881 works (lower quality is expected).
- [ ] **(VPS)** Spike D1 on real hardware: with one 1080p30 publisher and 1, 3 and 5 real viewers, publisher upload stays flat and server CPU stays low (18 §K lists what to record).

## G. Custom resolution, frame rate and bitrate

Share screen → dialog, and Settings → Voice & audio → Camera, both have **Custom** next to the presets, with resolution (360p to 2160p/4K), frame rate (5 to 60) and bitrate (Auto, or a value in Mbit/s). The media test covers a custom 1440p30 camera (VP9, upload flat from 1 to 2 viewers, none with no viewer), a custom 1080p60 share and a custom 2160p15 share on a canvas, with the readout of asked against delivered. **Not tried anywhere: a real 4K display, a real 4K or 1440p camera, Firefox, a slow machine, a viewer that cannot decode VP9.**

- [ ] Pick a preset: the controls show its values. Edit the frame rate: Custom is selected and the resolution stays. Pick a preset again: the controls follow it, and Custom remembers your values for next time.
- [ ] Bitrate "Auto" shows the figure it uses (about 1.65 Mbit/s for 720p30, 3.75 for 1080p30, 15 for 4K30). "Set a value" takes a number in Mbit/s.
- [ ] With the server default caps (6 Mbit/s screen, 1.7 camera) choose 4K: the text says "Capped to 6 Mbit/s by the server or channel, expect blockiness" and a button offers the highest resolution that fits; pressing it changes the controls.
- [ ] Settings → Server → Video quality: raise the screen share cap to 40 and the camera cap to 12, **rejoin the call** (caps apply from the next join), and pick 4K again: the warning is gone and a second one says it needs a fast computer and roughly the shown upload. The same card accepts up to 60 and 25 Mbit/s and refuses more.
- [ ] A channel's own screen share cap (channel settings) still lowers the server's: set 3 Mbit/s on one channel and the capped text appears there only.
- [ ] **(you)** Share a **real 4K screen** at 2160p, 15 or 30 fps, with a cap of 40 Mbit/s. The sharing bar says what was asked for, what the browser captured and what is sent, and "The encoder is limited by your processor" appears if the computer cannot keep up. Note the CPU use and the real upload (`chrome://webrtc-internals` or the task manager).
- [ ] **(you)** Share a window or a tab smaller than the request: the readout says "Your browser delivers less than asked for" (the browser cannot upscale).
- [ ] **(you)** A real camera at 1440p or 1080p60 if it supports it; a camera that only does 1080p shows "Captured 1920×1080" against "Asked for 2560×1440".
- [ ] **(people)** A viewer on a laptop watches the 4K share with Auto: the picture is the size of the tile (a lower layer), High asks for the full picture and downloads about the shown bitrate; Pause video stops it.
- [ ] **(Firefox)** Camera at 720p30 and Custom 1080p30 work; Firefox may not offer VP9 for sending, then the setting falls back to VP8 and the readout says VP8.
- [ ] **(you)** Change resolution or frame rate while sharing (sharing bar → Change): the picture keeps going, the readout updates; the codec cannot change without a new capture, so a jump from 1080p30 (VP8) to 4K stays on VP8 until you stop and start again.
- [ ] **(VPS)** With a 20 Mbit/s 1080p60 share and three real viewers, watch the server's egress (about 3 times the shown bitrate at most) and CPU.
