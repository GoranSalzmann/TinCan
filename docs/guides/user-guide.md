# Tin Can user guide (draft, M5)

For members. Your server admin runs the server; this is about using it.

## Getting in

Open the address your admin gave you. With an invite link, choose a username and a password of at least 10 characters (a passphrase of a few words is fine). If you forget it, ask your admin for a one-time reset code and use **Use a reset code** on the login screen. There is no email reset.

Sign-ins are listed under Settings › My account; **revoke** any you do not recognise.

## Text

Channels are on the left. Write with simple formatting: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `~~strike~~`, `` `code` ``, fenced code blocks, `> quotes`, lists, `||spoiler||`. Mention people with `@name`, a role with `@role`, a channel with `#channel`. Links open in a new tab; if a link's text shows one site but goes to another, you are asked first.

Files: drop or paste into the message box. Images show inline; everything else downloads. You can see and delete your own uploads under Settings › Storage.

## Voice and video

Click a voice channel to join; click the microphone or headphones to mute or deafen yourself. Settings › Voice & audio has your devices, noise suppression, push to talk and a connection test. Camera and screen share are in the call bar; nobody receives your screen or camera picture until they press **Watch**, and you can see who is watching.

### The lock

A lock and "End-to-end encrypted" in the call header mean the **media server cannot read the call**. Be clear about what it does not mean:

- Your server admin manages the keys, so they could in principle listen. Pick a server you trust.
- Everyone in the call can record it.
- Who is in the call, when, and who is speaking are visible to the server.
- Text messages and files are **not** end-to-end encrypted.

If your browser cannot do encrypted calls you are told so; try a current Chrome or Edge.

## Moderation, from your side

Moderators can time you out (you can read but not write or speak until it ends), mute you in voice (it stays until they lift it), move you to another voice channel, or remove you from the server. If you are banned the login screen tells you, with the reason if one was given. These actions are recorded in a log that moderators and admins can read; it does not include message text.

## Staying safe

Do not share your password or reset code; admins never need it. Treat invite links like keys: anyone with the link can join. Report problems to your admin.
