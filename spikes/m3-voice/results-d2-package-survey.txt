  ships: rnnoise.wasm 152 kB, rnnoise_simd.wasm 157 kB, speex.wasm 56 kB, gtcrn.wasm 197 kB + AudioWorklet processors.

deepfilternet3-noise-filter 1.3.0        Apache-2.0 OR MIT   unpacked 73 kB
  contains only a loader. WASM and model are fetched at run time from https://cdn.mezon.ai/AI/models/datas/noise_suppression/deepfilternet3
  (config.cdnUrl). Not acceptable as shipped: a third-party host in the audio path, nothing to hash or reproduce.
  A self-hosted DeepFilterNet needs a build of upstream (Rikorose/DeepFilterNet, MIT or Apache-2.0) to WASM with its model weights.
